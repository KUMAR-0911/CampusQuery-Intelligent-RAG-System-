Q1. Who chairs the Institute Standing Disciplinary Committee (ISDC), and who is its convenor?
A1. The Dean (Student Welfare) is the Chairperson, and the Chief Warden is the Convenor.
Q2. Under which provision of the NIT Act 2007 does the Director hold responsibility for keeping order at the institute?
A2. Article 17(2) of the NIT Act 2007. The Director may also invoke clause 17(2) in rare cases to decide a student disciplinary issue on his own.
Q3. What is the maximum financial penalty that can be imposed as a punishment, and what is the recovery rate for damage to Institute property?
A3. The financial penalty is up to Rs. 5,000/-. For damage to Institute property caused by irresponsible behavior, the cost is recovered at 3 times the replacement cost, in addition to other disciplinary measures.
Q4. Within how many days must a decision be made after a disciplinary case is reported, and within how many days can a student appeal?
A4. The decision must be made within 7 days of reporting, and the student has 7 days from the decision to appeal.
Q5. What are the four stages of the disciplinary process shown in the "Steps Involved" flowchart?
A5. Report, Investigate, Decide, and Appeal.
Q6. What happens to a student caught adopting unfair means in a mid/end-term examination, and what happens if the student repeats the offense?
A6. The student is awarded an 'F' grade in the subject(s) concerned and is reported to a counseling committee. If the student repeats the offense, he/she will be rusticated from the institute.
Q7. Is a junior student who suffers ragging but does not report it free from punishment?
A7. No. If a junior student yields to ragging and does not inform the institute or hostel authorities, or willfully withholds information in an investigation, this is treated as indiscipline and attracts punishments comparable to those for ragging. Suppression of a complaint by a junior does not exempt the senior from punishment.
Q8. Which punishments are classified as "major punishments," and what additional consequence do they carry?
A8. Punishments under items (v) to (ix): partial or complete debarment from campus placement, academic suspension for one or two consecutive semesters, expulsion from the hostel, rustication for a specified period, and outright expulsion from the Institute. They debar a student from all academic medals and prizes, as well as important non-academic awards.
Q9. What are the hostel rules about leaving the hostel at night for boys, and what is the maximum mess leave allowed per month?
A9. Boys are not allowed to stay outside the hostel from 10:00 PM to 4:30 AM for any reason except medical issues. A boarder can take a maximum of 8 days of leave from the mess in a month, with approval from the hostel authority.
Q10. How many late entries lead to a warning letter for a hosteller?
A10. After 3 late entries, one warning letter is issued. If the student is late despite the warning, it leads to disciplinary action.
Q11. What are the visiting rules for guardians at Gargi Hostel?
A11. Only lady guardians may visit the boarders, on Saturdays, Sundays, and Institute holidays, from 12:30 PM to 3:30 PM, for 30 minutes (or as permitted by the hostel authority), after submitting a photocopy of a government-issued identity proof.
Q12. Which deterrents are automatically added to all punishments from P-3 onwards?
A12. P-1 (warning with undertaking not to repeat the mistake) is automatically added to all punishments from P-2 onwards, and P-2 (parents intimated) is automatically added to all punishments from P-3 onwards. So both P-1 and P-2 apply from P-3 onwards.
Note on Q12: the manual's notes contain a small inconsistency (Note 1 says P-1 is added from P-2 onwards, Note 2 says P-2 is added from P-3 onwards), so the answer reflects both notes combined.
Q13. What deterrents are prescribed for sexual harassment of a fellow student (OA-35) and for possessing explosives or firearms (OA-42)?
A13. OA-35 (sexual harassment) carries P15/P16. OA-42 (possessing explosives, firearms or such dangerous items) carries P16/P17.
Q14. What penalty applies to a student who makes a false complaint against another student?
A14. The penalty is at least one step higher than the highest penalty recommended for the offense charged.
Q15. What is the Director's power regarding punishments recommended by the ISDC?
A15. The Director may approve, return to ISDC for re-evaluation, reduce the punishment, or submit the matter to the Senate. The Director may make minor changes to the nature of punishment or reduce its level/quantum, but shall not increase the punishment recommended by the ISDC.
Q16. What is the deterrent for a fresher not reporting a ragging incident OB-1 to OB-5 involving oneself (OC-2)?
A16. P1-P3.
