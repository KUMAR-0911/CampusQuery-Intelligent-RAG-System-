To be a globally renowned academic institution that fosters innovation through the advancement of science and technology. We pursue high-quality teaching and research in alignment with national missions, and nurture future-ready graduates.

Our Mission
We are committed to delivering high-quality teaching that empowers students to develop future-ready, critical thinking skills.
We shall encourage high-quality research in science and technology that drives meaningful societal impact.
We aim to design and sustain industry-aligned programmes and research that will aid students and faculty in contributing to cutting-edge innovation.
We are dedicated to creating an ecosystem of high-quality faculty, officers, staff and scholars who engage with emerging and exponential technologies through top-tier research.
We strive to leverage NIT Agartala’s locational advantage to be able to attract high-quality talent from across the country and internationally.
We shall prioritize sustainability by encouraging clean and green technology.
We will focus on cautious scaling, prioritizing both growth and quality as we continue on our journey to establishing ourselves as globally renowned academic institution.
Core Values
Promote scientific temper to foster a culture of innovation.
Uphold high standards of honesty, hard work and discipline.
Sustain an ecosystem that rewards responsible and ethical behaviour.
Build a culture based on transparency, fairness, equity and mutual respect.
Provide intellectual autonomy to faculty and scholars in pursuit of excellence.
Our Education System
Presently the Institute offers eight UG courses and PG courses in several fields along with Post Doctoral Fellowships. This Institute is autonomous under NIT being act passed by the parliament.

The Institute recently introduced a new curriculum in the line of IITs where more number of elective subjects are being offered to make the system flexible. Previously, the Institute used to follow the mark based system of evaluation, but have been switching over to credit based system in order to keep track with the global evaluation methods. Industrial training, Undertaking projects are parts of the education system and students are also encouraged to take various activities for social development through NSS and NCC.
