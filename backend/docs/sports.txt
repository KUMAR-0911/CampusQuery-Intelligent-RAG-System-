Sports activities at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Agartala are centered around the Atal Bihari Vajpayee (ABV) Sports Complex and governed by the Student Discipline Manual, the Sports Section, and the All India Inter-NIT Sports Board.
While specific facility notices (such as court-booking slots and seasonal gym timetables) are updated regularly on the NIT Agartala Official Notice Board, general sports regulations fall into four main categories:

1. Facility Access & Operating Rules

• Operating Hours and Timetables. Access to the indoor stadiums, outdoor grounds, swimming pool, and gymnasium is strictly regulated by designated operating hours. Separate time slots are allocated for boys and girls—particularly for facilities like the indoor swimming pool and gym—and are periodically updated via circulars from the Sports Section.
• Priority Usage. Regular recreational access is generally scheduled outside academic hours. Scheduled institute team practices, Physical Education/Yoga sessions, and major campus tournaments take priority over casual play, and facilities may be closed to general access during holidays (e.g., Puja holidays) or inter-college events.
• Mandatory Sports Attire. Proper sportswear—specifically athletic shoes (non-marking shoes for indoor wooden/synthetic courts), t-shirts, and lowers/shorts—is compulsory when entering courts, fields, or training sessions.

2. General Conduct & Discipline

Under the NIT Agartala Student Discipline Manual, students using sports infrastructure must adhere to strict behavioral standards:
• Respect for Officials. Students must follow all instructions issued by the Student Activity & Sports (SAS) Officer, Physical Education Officers, coaches, and campus security personnel.
• Equipment and Property Care. Damage to sports equipment, indoor flooring, or stadium infrastructure due to negligence or misconduct is subject to disciplinary action and fines.
• Anti-Ragging and Inclusivity. Seniors and juniors share practice spaces during orientations and trials (such as Gymkhana selections), and students are required to maintain a welcoming, ragging-free environment on all grounds.

3. Institute Team Selection & Intra-Campus Tournaments

• Gymkhana & Club Trials. Institute teams for cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, badminton, table tennis, lawn tennis, and hockey are selected through official trials announced at the start of the academic session.
• Tournament Eligibility. Intra-college events—including Inter-Year, Inter-Branch, and First-Year tournaments (such as the Entrance Cup)—require valid student ID registration and adherence to tournament-specific formats set by the NITA Student Gymkhana and sports clubs.

4. Inter-NIT & External Competition Rules

For students representing NIT Agartala in the All India Inter-NIT Sports Tournaments or All India Inter-University (AIU) events:
• Medical Insurance & Fitness. All participating athletes must hold valid medical insurance provided by the institute and meet the eligibility criteria outlined in the AIU Sports Handbook and Inter-NIT Sports Board guidelines.
• Host Medical Standards. When NIT Agartala hosts Inter-NIT events, tournament rules mandate on-site medical personnel, including a doctor, physiotherapist, first-aid team, and ambulance services throughout the competition.
For specific court bookings, current gym schedules, or equipment issuance rules, you can contact the Sports Section directly via the contacts listed on the Sports at NIT Agartala portal.
